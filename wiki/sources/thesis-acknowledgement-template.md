# Thesis Acknowledgement Section Template

**Created**: 2026-04-17  
**Format**: ITC Cambodia standard  
**Page Number**: i (lowercase roman numeral)  
**Page Count**: Typically 1-2 pages

---

## Acknowledgement Structure

The acknowledgement section follows the cover pages and comes before the Abstract. It is numbered with lowercase roman numerals.

### Formatting Rules
- **Header**: "ACKNOWLEDGEMENT" (all caps, centered, 12pt bold)
- **Font**: Times New Roman, 12pt, body text
- **Spacing**: 1.5 or double line spacing
- **Alignment**: Justified (both left and right edges)
- **Paragraphs**: 3-5 paragraphs typical
- **Length**: 300-500 words
- **Footer**: "Page i of [Total pages]" (centered, bottom of page)

### Paragraph Structure
1. **Opening paragraph**: General gratitude and acknowledgment of support
2. **Academic/Advisor paragraph**: Specific thanks to thesis advisor and co-advisor
3. **Professional/Internship paragraph**: Thanks to industry supervisor and colleagues
4. **Committee/Institutional paragraph**: Thanks to committee members and institution
5. **Personal paragraph**: Gratitude to family and friends
6. **Closing paragraph**: Final reflection on journey (optional)

Last but not least, I would like to thank my beloved family and friends for their unwavering 
support, understanding, and motivation. Their encouragement has been a constant source of strength 
and inspiration throughout my academic journey.

## Key Elements to Include

### Essential Mentions (in order)
1. ✓ Academic advisor (with full title/name)
2. ✓ Co-advisor (if applicable)
3. ✓ Industry supervisor/mentor (if internship-based)
4. ✓ Thesis committee members (optional but recommended)
5. ✓ Department head
6. ✓ Institution/Rector
7. ✓ Department secretary/administrative staff
8. ✓ Family and friends
9. ✓ Other colleagues who contributed

### Tone Guidelines
- **Formal but warm**: Show genuine appreciation without being overly casual
- **Specific**: Reference specific contributions, not just generic thanks
- **Humble**: Acknowledge others' role in your success
- **Concise**: Get to the point; don't ramble
- **Sincere**: This is not just a formality—express real gratitude

## Common Phrases to Use

### For Advisors
- "I would like to express my sincere gratitude to..."
- "...for their invaluable guidance and unwavering support..."
- "Their expertise, patience, and dedication have been vital..."
- "...for their continuous support throughout this research journey..."

### For Supervisors
- "I am deeply grateful to my industry supervisor..."
- "...for their professional mentorship and technical support..."
- "His practical insights significantly enriched..."
- "...for providing access to [resource/data/equipment]..."

### For Committee Members
- "I am deeply thankful to the members of my thesis committee..."
- "...for their time, thoughtful evaluation, and constructive feedback..."
- "...which have significantly improved the clarity and quality..."

### For Department/Institution
- "I extend my appreciation to the Department of..."
- "I am grateful to [Department] for fostering a supportive environment..."
- "I also wish to acknowledge [staff member] for their administrative assistance..."

### For Family/Friends
- "Last but not least, I would like to thank my family and friends..."
- "Their encouragement has been a constant source of strength..."
- "For all they have done, I will always be grateful..."
