# ITC Cambodia Thesis Formatting Guide

**Created**: 2026-04-17  
**Source**: Final Draft Thesis Year 5 by Tyda.pdf, Offical_Thesis_Vannak_Vireakyuth.pdf, DORN_Dawin_Thesis_Final_Reported.pdf  
**Institution**: Institut de Technologie du Cambodge (ITC) — Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics  
**Language**: Bilingual (Khmer + French/English)

---

## Document-Level Specifications

### Font & Spacing
- **Font Face**: Times New Roman, 12pt (body text)
- **Headings**: Times New Roman, 14-16pt bold
- **Line Spacing**: 1.5 or double-spaced throughout document
- **Margins**: 1 inch (2.54 cm) on all sides
- **Alignment**: Justified (both left and right edges aligned)
- **Page Numbering**: 
  - Front matter: lowercase roman numerals (i, ii, iii, iv, v, vi, vii, viii, ix, x)
  - Main content: arabic numerals (1, 2, 3...) starting from first chapter
  - Page number placement: bottom center or top right corner

### Paper Format
- **Page Size**: A4 (210 × 297 mm)
- **Color**: Black and white throughout (including logos/seals)
- **Print Quality**: Professional (300 DPI minimum for figures/tables)

## Front Matter Order (Before Main Chapters)

### 1. Cover Page (Page 1, not numbered)
Standard ITC cover page with:
- Ministry of Education seal (top left)
- ITC seal (top right)
- Khmer title (centered, large)
- French/English title (centered, large)
- "MINISTERE DE L'EDUCATION, DE LA JEUNESSE ET DES SPORTS"
- "INSTITUT DE TECHNOLOGIE DU CAMBODGE"
- Department name in both languages
- Thesis type: "MEMOIRE DE FIN D'ETUDES" or "RAPPORT DE STAGE D'INGENIEUR"
- Student name, Speciality, Advisor name, Year (formatted in table structure)

### 2. Back Cover / Title Page (Page 2, not numbered)
Mirror of front cover with official authorization box:
- Same header information
- Large ITC seal (watermark style in background)
- Thesis title
- Student information
- Defense date: "Date de soutenance: le [DATE] [MONTH] [YEAR]"
- Authorization phrase: "« Autorise la soutenance du mémoire »"
- Approving signatures section with blanks for:
  - Director of Institute
  - Department Head
  - Thesis Advisor
  - Internship Supervisor (if applicable)
  - Responsible Officer

### 3. Acknowledgement (Page i)
- **Header**: "ACKNOWLEDGEMENT" (all caps)
- **Content**: 3-4 paragraphs, justified
- **Style**: Formal, expressing gratitude to advisor, supervisors, family, committee members
- **Language**: English (for English theses) or French (for French theses)
- **Footer**: "Page i of [Total Pages]"

### 4. Khmer Acknowledgement (Page ii) [if bilingual]
- **Header**: "សូត្របាតរដ្ឋ" (Khmer equivalent)
- **Content**: Khmer version of acknowledgement
- **Footer**: "Page ii of [Total Pages]"

### 5. Abstract (Page iii)
- **Header**: "ABSTRACT" (all caps)
- **Content**: 1-2 pages, single-spaced or 1.5-spaced paragraphs
- **Length**: 250-400 words summarizing thesis objective, methodology, key findings, and impact
- **Structure**: Problem → Solution → Methodology → Results → Implications
- **Language**: English

### 6. Résumé (Page iv) [if bilingual]
- **Header**: "RÉSUMÉ" (all caps)
- **Content**: French summary equivalent to Abstract
- **Length**: 250-400 words

### 7. Khmer Summary (Page v) [if trilingual]
- **Header**: "សង្ខេប" (Khmer word for "summary")
- **Content**: Khmer summary

### 8. Table of Contents (Page vi)
- **Header**: "TABLE OF CONTENTS"
- **Format**: Hierarchical list with page numbers
- **Structure**: 
  - Chapter numbers and titles (all caps or Title Case)
  - Section numbers and titles (e.g., 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, etc.)
  - Subsection numbers and titles (e.g., 1.1.1, 1.1.2)
- **Indentation**: Increasing indent per level
- **Leaders**: Dots connecting title to page number
- **Example**:
  ```
  CHAPTER I. INTRODUCTION ................................................... 1
    1.1    Problem Statement ..................................................... 1
    1.2    Objectives ............................................................. 2
  CHAPTER II. LITERATURE REVIEW .......................................... 4
    2.1    Related Work ........................................................... 4
    2.2    Gaps in Current Knowledge .......................................... 5
  ```

### 9. List of Figures (Page vii)
- **Header**: "LIST OF FIGURES"
- **Format**: Figure number, caption, page number
- **Example**: "Figure 4.1: PSI Response to Population Distortion (0%-50%)     .... 40"

### 10. List of Tables (Page viii)
- **Header**: "LIST OF TABLES"
- **Format**: Table number, caption, page number

### 11. List of Abbreviations (Page ix) [optional]
- **Header**: "LIST OF ABBREVIATIONS" or "ACRONYMS"
- **Format**: Two columns (Abbreviation : Full Term)
- **Example**:
  ```
  PSI       :  Population Stability Index
  MR        :  Mortality Ratio
  HITL      :  Human-In-The-Loop
  ```

## Main Content Chapter Structure

### Chapter Heading Format
```
CHAPTER [NUMBER]. [TITLE IN ALL CAPS]

[Page break]

[Section heading]

[Body text starts here...]
```

### Section Headings Hierarchy

**Level 1 - Chapter Header** (All Caps, 16pt)
```
CHAPTER I. INTRODUCTION
```

**Level 2 - Main Section** (Title Case, 14pt, bold)
```
1.1 Background and Motivation
```

**Level 3 - Subsection** (Title Case, 12pt, bold)
```
1.1.1 Problem Context
```

**Level 4 - Sub-subsection** (Title Case, 12pt, italic)
```
a) Historical perspective
```

### Paragraph Formatting
- **First line indent**: 0.5 inches (1.27 cm)
- **Spacing between paragraphs**: None (use line spacing to separate)
- **Spacing after headings**: One blank line

### Body Text Guidelines
- **Font**: Times New Roman, 12pt
- **Alignment**: Justified
- **Line spacing**: 1.5 or double
- **Text width**: approximately 6-6.5 inches (16-17 cm) given 1-inch margins

## Figures, Tables, and Captions

### Figure Placement
- Figures should be embedded in-text near where they are referenced
- Figures should be centered on the page
- Minimum size: 3 × 3 inches; Maximum: 6 × 7 inches (accounting for margins)

### Figure Caption Format
```
Figure [Number]. [Brief Title Describing Figure Content]

[If needed, a note about methodology or data source]
```
- **Placement**: Below the figure
- **Font**: Times New Roman, 10pt (smaller than body)
- **Example**: `Figure 4.1: PSI Response to Population Distortion (0%-50%)`

### Table Placement
- Tables should be embedded in-text near where they are referenced
- Tables should be centered on the page

### Table Caption Format
```
Table [Number]. [Brief Title Describing Table Content]

[Table content]

[If needed, a note about data source]
```
- **Placement**: Above the table
- **Font**: Times New Roman, 10pt
- **Example**: `Table 5.1: PSI Values by Distortion Fraction`

### Figure and Table Numbering
- Numbers are sequential within each chapter: Figure 1.1, 1.2, 1.3... 2.1, 2.2, etc.
- Same for tables: Table 1.1, 1.2... 2.1, 2.2, etc.

## Citations and References

### In-Text Citation Style
- **Author-Date System** (parenthetical): (Smith 2020) or Smith (2020) argues that...
- For multiple authors: (Smith and Jones 2020) or (Smith et al. 2020)
- Direct quote: (Smith 2020, p. 45) states "direct quote here"

### Reference List Format
- **Header**: "References" or "Bibliography"
- **Placement**: End of thesis, before appendices
- **Alphabetical order**: By author last name
- **Hanging indent**: 0.5 inches
- **Font**: Times New Roman, 12pt
- **Line spacing**: Single-spaced within entries, double-spaced between entries

### Example Reference Formats

**Book**:
```
Smith, J. A. (2020). Title of the book. Publisher Name.
```

**Journal Article**:
```
Jones, M. B., & Brown, C. D. (2019). Title of article. Journal Title, 42(3), 123-145.
```

**Conference Paper**:
```
Davis, R. E. (2021). Title of paper. In Proceedings of Conference Name (pp. 100-110).
```

**Thesis/Dissertation**:
```
Wilson, K. L. (2018). Title of thesis. Master's thesis, Institut de Technologie du Cambodge.
```

**Website/URL**:
```
Green, T. J. (2022). Title of webpage. Retrieved from https://www.example.com/page
```

## Special Sections

### Acknowledgement Content Guidelines
- Express sincere gratitude to thesis advisor and co-advisor
- Thank industry supervisors and mentors
- Acknowledge committee members
- Thank family and friends for support
- 300-500 words typical
- Formal but personal tone

### Abstract Content Guidelines
- Lead with the problem statement
- State your specific research question or hypothesis
- Describe your methodology briefly
- Summarize key findings/results
- Discuss implications or impact
- 250-400 words typical
- Third person, formal tone
- No citations in abstract

### Introduction Content Guidelines
- **Section 1.1**: Motivate the problem (why it matters)
- **Section 1.2**: State the research question or thesis statement
- **Section 1.3**: List specific objectives or contributions
- **Section 1.4**: Outline the structure of the thesis
- **Typical length**: 3-5 pages
- **Tone**: Engaging but formal

### Methodology Content Guidelines
- Describe the data or participants (source, size, selection method)
- Explain your research design (experimental, observational, computational, etc.)
- Detail procedures and protocols
- Define key variables and measurements
- Describe analysis methods
- **Typical length**: 4-8 pages
- **Include**: Diagrams, flowcharts, or process maps if helpful

### Results Content Guidelines
- Present findings without interpretation
- Use tables and figures to summarize quantitative results
- Report statistics or metrics relevant to objectives
- Organize by research question or hypothesis
- **Typical length**: 3-10 pages (varies by complexity)
- **Include**: Visualizations (graphs, charts, heatmaps)

### Discussion Content Guidelines
- Interpret results in context of literature
- Explain how findings answer research questions
- Discuss limitations of the study
- Suggest implications for theory and practice
- Recommend future research directions
- **Typical length**: 3-8 pages

### Conclusion Content Guidelines
- Summarize main contributions and findings
- Restate the broader significance
- Connect back to original motivation
- Outline next steps or future work
- **Typical length**: 1-3 pages

## Common Formatting Issues to Avoid

❌ **Don't**:
- Mix fonts (stick with Times New Roman throughout)
- Use single spacing (use 1.5 or double)
- Left-align body text (justify instead)
- Number the cover page or back cover
- Mix citation styles (pick one and stick to it)
- Use footnotes excessively (prefer endnotes or in-text citations)
- Embed images as separate files (embed them in the document)
- Use inconsistent heading styles
- Skip page numbering on front matter
- Indent the first paragraph of a section

✅ **Do**:
- Use consistent 12pt Times New Roman for body
- Double-check that all page numbers are present and sequential
- Verify all figures and tables are referenced in text
- Check that all abbreviations are defined on first use
- Ensure all in-text citations have corresponding references
- Proofread for spelling, grammar, and punctuation
- Have someone else review the formatting
- Create a PDF version for final submission

## File Format and Submission

- **Format**: PDF (for official submission)
- **Page Count**: Typically 40-100 pages (excluding appendices)
- **File Size**: Under 50 MB recommended
- **File Naming**: `YourName_Thesis_2026.pdf` or `Thesis_YourName_AMS_2026.pdf`
- **Encoding**: UTF-8 for compatibility with Khmer text

---

## Bilingual/Trilingual Considerations

For theses in multiple languages:
1. **Front Cover**: Khmer + French headers
2. **Back Cover/Title Page**: Khmer + French + English information
3. **Acknowledgements**: English version, then Khmer version (separate pages)
4. **Abstract**: English (page iii) + Résumé in French (page iv) + Khmer Summary (page v)
5. **Main Chapters**: Typically in one language (e.g., English), with key terms defined in Khmer
6. **Appendices**: Bilingual labels ("APPENDIX A: ..." in English headers, with Khmer labels if needed)
