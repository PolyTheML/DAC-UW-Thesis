## Specific Field Explanations

### Header Information

**MINISTERE DE L'EDUCATION, DE LA JEUNESSE ET DES SPORTS**
- This is the Cambodian Ministry of Education, Youth, and Sports
- Always include exactly as shown

**INSTITUT DE TECHNOLOGIE DU CAMBODGE**
- Full official name of the institution
- Can use "Institut de Technologie du Cambodge" (French) or "Institute of Technology of Cambodia" (English)

### Department Names

**For Data Science / Mathematics & Statistics specialization**:
- French: "DEPARTMENT DE MATHEMATIQUES APPLIQUEES ET STATISTIQUES"
- English: "DEPARTMENT OF APPLIED MATHEMATICS AND STATISTICS"

### Thesis Type

**MEMOIRE DE FIN D'ETUDES**
- Standard final year thesis (Mémoire = Memory/Thesis in French)
- Use for Year 5 final projects

**RAPPORT DE STAGE D'INGENIEUR DE CINQUIEME ANNEE**
- Internship report for 5th year engineering students
- Used if thesis is based on internship work

### Student Information Fields

**Etudiant / Etudiante**:
- Use "Etudiant" for male students
- Use "Etudiante" for female students
- Or use gender-neutral "Etudiant(e)" or "Student"

**Spécialité**:
- "Data Science" (for data science focus)
- "Applied Mathematics and Statistics" (for mathematics focus)
- "Computer Science" (for CS focus)
- "Engineering in Applied Mathematics and Statistics" (full program name)

**Tuteur de stage**:
- Title: "Dr." if advisor has PhD, "Eng." if engineer, "Mr./Ms." if other
- Format: "Dr. HAS Sothea" or "Dr. LIN Mongkolsery"

**Année scolaire**:
- Format: "2024-2025" or "2025-2026"
- Includes the academic year span

### Back Cover Fields

**Date de soutenance**:
- Use: "Date de soutenance: le 26 Juin 2026"
- Format: "le [DD] [MONTH NAME] [YEAR]" (day spelled out, month in French)
- Example months: Janvier, Février, Mars, Avril, Mai, Juin, Juillet, Août, Septembre, Octobre, Novembre, Décembre

**Directeur de l'Institut**:
- Typically: "Dr. PO Kimtho" (current ITC Director)
- Or latest director at your defense date

**Phnom Penh, le _______**:
- Leave blank for your advisor/committee to fill in during defense

**Etablissement du stage**:
- Company or organization where internship was conducted
- Example: "Phillip Bank PLC", "Factory AI", "Ministry of Education"

**Chef du département**:
- Head of AMS Department
- Check current department head (typically "Dr. LIN Mongkolsery")

**Responsable de l'établissement**:
- Supervisor at the internship company/institution
- Example: "Mr. MEAN Soknan" or "Dr. KHUN Kimang"

## Formatting Rules for Cover Page

### Typography
- **Font**: Times New Roman throughout
- **Title (Khmer)**: 16pt, bold, centered
- **Department**: 14pt, bold, centered
- **Subtitle**: 12pt, centered
- **Field labels**: 12pt, regular weight
- **Field values**: 12pt, bold (for student name, etc.)

### Layout
- All text centered except for signature blocks
- Use horizontal lines (═══) to separate major sections visually
- Leave approximately 1.5 inch margins on all sides
- Center the seal images (Ministry + ITC) at top

### Signature Block (Back Cover)
```
                            PHNOM PENH, le _______ 20__

                    Approved by:

                    ___________________________
                    Dr. [ADVISOR NAME]
                    Thesis Advisor
```

Or (if multiple reviewers):
```
                    Examined and approved by:

                    ___________________________     ___________________________
                    Dr. [COMMITTEE MEMBER 1]       Dr. [COMMITTEE MEMBER 2]

                    ___________________________
                    Dr. [THESIS ADVISOR]
```

## Print Specifications

- **Page Size**: A4 (210 × 297 mm / 8.27 × 11.69 inches)
- **Margins**: 1 inch (2.54 cm) on all sides
- **Background**: White
- **Ink Color**: Black (for official submission)
- **Print Quality**: 300 DPI or higher
- **Paper Weight**: 80 gsm (standard white paper) for most pages, optionally 120 gsm for cover if binding

---

## Notes

1. **Khmer Text**: If you need to include Khmer text, ensure your document uses a font that supports Khmer Unicode (Times New Roman supports Khmer in modern versions)
2. **Seals**: Contact ITC to request official Ministry and ITC seals as image files
3. **Advisor Name**: Double-check spelling and credentials (Dr., Eng., etc.)
4. **Defense Date**: This will be finalized by your department closer to your thesis submission date
5. **Bilingual Option**: If writing in both English and French, ensure both appear clearly and equally prominently
