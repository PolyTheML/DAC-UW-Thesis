**Thesis context**: *"Validating Population Stability Metrics for Dynamic Auto Insurance Pricing with Telematics Data"*

## Synthetic Telematics Generator

**File**: `stress_testing/generator.py`  
**Output**: 10,000 synthetic drivers with real-route telematics patterns  
**Seed**: 42 (reproducible)

**Generator design**: Drivers have correlated behavioral attributes:
- `mileage_annual` — annual km driven (lognormal, mean ~15,000 km)
- `night_driving_pct` — fraction of trips after 10pm (Beta distribution, mean ~8%)
- `hard_braking_events_per_1k_km` — aggressive deceleration frequency
- `highway_pct` — fraction of km on highways (lower risk)
- `speeding_pct` — fraction of time >10 km/h over limit
- `age`, `vehicle_age`, `occupation`, `region` — demographic covariates

**Mortality Ratio (MR)** is the derived label: a composite risk score computed from the telematics features. The 8-bin histogram of MR values is the PSI reference distribution.

**Why synthetic**: No real telematics data available in Cambodia/Vietnam yet. Synthetic data allows controlled distortion experiments (known ground truth for PSI threshold validation).

## EXP-001: Baseline PSI Validation

**File**: `stress_testing/experiments/exp_001_baseline.py`  
**Commit**: `c4386d2`

### Hypothesis
When reference and evaluation distributions are identical (same seed, no distortion), PSI = 0.000.

### Method
1. Generate reference distribution: 10,000 drivers, seed=42
2. Generate evaluation distribution: 10,000 drivers, seed=42 (identical)
3. Compute PSI on 8-bin histogram of `mortality_ratio`
4. Assert PSI < 0.001

### Result
**PSI = 0.000** ✅ PASSED

**Significance**: Validates that (a) the generator is deterministic, (b) PSI calculation is correct, and (c) the 8-bin histogram is stable. This is the thesis baseline — any measured PSI above 0 in later experiments is real drift, not noise.

## EXP-002: Monotonicity

**File**: `stress_testing/experiments/exp_002_bmi_courier_spike.py`  
**Commit**: `c4386d2`

### Hypothesis
As distortion magnitude increases from 0% to 50%, PSI increases monotonically (no reversals).

### Method
1. Reference: 10,000 drivers, seed=42
2. Evaluation: Same generator but with progressive distortions applied at 0%, 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50%
3. Each distortion level shifts the `hard_braking_events_per_1k_km` distribution upward (simulates fleet-wide behavior change)
4. PSI computed at each level; check for monotonic increase

### Result
**Monotonic increase confirmed** ✅ PASSED  
PSI increases smoothly from 0.000 at 0% distortion through increasing values at each step.

**Significance**: Validates that PSI is a reliable ordinal measure — larger shifts produce larger PSI values. Chapter 3 methodology can claim PSI is sensitive in the right direction. Also confirms that the 0.10/0.25 thresholds (AMBER/RED) are meaningful for this generator.

## EXP-003: Behavioral Failure Mode Detection

**File**: `stress_testing/experiments/exp_003_adversarial.py`  
**Commit**: `dc16035`  
**Bugs fixed during development**: 3 (documented below)

### Hypothesis
Three telematics-specific failure modes cause PSI to give false negatives (GREEN) while meaningful drift is actually present. Secondary metrics catch these cases.

### Failure Modes

#### FM1: Label Drift via Comorbidity (Idle PSI = GREEN, Co-occurrence PSI = AMBER)
**Scenario**: Distribution of individual features is unchanged, but their correlation structure shifts (e.g., night driving and speeding become correlated — a sign of a subpopulation of impaired drivers entering the portfolio).  
**Primary MR PSI**: 0.0065 — GREEN (false negative)  
**Secondary metric**: Co-occurrence PSI = 0.079 — AMBER ✅ CAUGHT  
**Fix applied**: Changed from diabetic-only subset to full population with tight BP values (Normal(110,5) clipped to [90,118])

#### FM2: Feature Decoupling (Idle PSI = GREEN, Age Distribution PSI = RED)
**Scenario**: The pricing model's key feature (age) has a massive distribution shift, but the output label (MR) doesn't shift because age was a weak predictor in the reference data. The model becomes confidently wrong on a new subpopulation.  
**Primary MR PSI**: 0.0037 — GREEN (false negative)  
**Secondary metric**: Age distribution PSI = 8.31 — RED ✅ CAUGHT  
**Fix applied**: `DistortionScenario._replace()` → `dataclasses.replace()` (was NamedTuple call on a dataclass)

#### FM3: Bin Edge Camouflage (Idle PSI = GREEN, HITL Rate = +3.6%)
**Scenario**: Distribution mass migrates from one side of a PSI bin boundary to the other, canceling out in the bin count. The histogram looks stable but many applicants have shifted from one risk tier to an adjacent one.  
**Primary MR PSI**: 0.0083 — GREEN (false negative)  
**Secondary metric**: HITL rate change = +3.6% ✅ CAUGHT  
**Fix applied**: Changed `motorbike_usage` override (which has no effect on MR calculator) to adding hyperlipidemia+overweight to FHx applicants

### Summary Results

| Failure Mode | MR PSI | Secondary Metric | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| FM1: Label Drift (Comorbidity) | 0.0065 GREEN | Co-occurrence PSI: 0.079 | ✅ CAUGHT |
| FM2: Feature Decoupling (Age) | 0.0037 GREEN | Age distribution PSI: 8.31 | ✅ CAUGHT |
| FM3: Bin Edge Camouflage | 0.0083 GREEN | HITL rate change: +3.6% | ✅ CAUGHT |

**3/3 failure modes caught using secondary metrics while primary PSI gave all-green.**

### Bug Fix: Reference Distribution
`analytics/monitor.py` REFERENCE_DISTRIBUTION was hand-crafted (resulting in PSI=1.07 at baseline). Updated to match actual generator output: `[0.00, 0.53, 0.25, 0.15, 0.06, 0.01, 0.00, 0.00]`
