Dataset Key Fields Format Frequency

Staying Accurate Over Time

Automated Retraining Pipeline

Model degrades as risk profiles evolve — we catch it automatically

1

Drift Detection (PSI Monitor)

PSI calculated daily vs. training baseline. Triggers retraining automatically.

PSI > 0.10 → Warning PSI > 0.25 → Auto-retrain
2

ETL & Feature Engineering

Pull latest 12 months of claims + policy data. Re-encode features. Validate schema.

3

Train Challenger Model

XGBoost retrained on fresh data. GLM refit with updated coefficients.

4

Champion vs. Challenger Evaluation

Compare Gini coefficient, RMSE, PSI on holdout set. Challenger must beat champion on all 3.

5

Promote or Reject

Challenger promoted → becomes new champion. Previous version archived with timestamp.

Promoted Rejected
6

Audit Log Written

Version, timestamp, trigger reason, model metrics — all recorded. Regulator-exportable.
