Closing

Four Original Contributions

01

Cambodia-Calibrated Dataset

2,000 synthetic applicants anchored on CDHS 2021-22, WHO STEPS 2023, and ILO 2023 statistics. Reproducible with seed 42.

02

Decoupled Fairness Architecture

PSI demographic monitoring + approval-rate parity as external guardrails. Preserves regret guarantees while enabling regulatory audit.

03

Human-in-the-Loop Wrapper

Dual updating on human override prevents the REFER arm from becoming a dead action. 6% reward lift at 2.5% human cost.

04

Reproducible Experimental Harness

Pre-registered hypotheses, fixed seeds, deterministic JSON reports, and CI-ready pass/fail criteria. Science, not storytelling.
